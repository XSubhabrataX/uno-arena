UNO Arena
Classic card battles for 2 to 7 players.

Play in the browser. No account and no network are required after the page loads.
Names, settings, and an in-progress match are stored on this device.

New game
  Choose 2 to 7 seats. Each seat can be a person on this device or an AI opponent
  (Easy, Normal, or Hard). Type any names you like.

Continue
  Picks up a saved match.

How a turn works
  Match the discard by color, number, or symbol. Wild cards are always allowed.
  Tap a card to lift it, then tap again to play it. You can also drag a card upward.
  Draw if you want, or when you have no legal card. A drawn card may be played
  immediately when it matches. Penalty cards may not.

Pass and play
  When more than one human is seated, the game covers the hand between turns.
  Hand the device over and press “I’m {name}” before the cards are shown.

Rules worth knowing
  Reverse with two players acts as Skip.
  Draw cards do not stack.
  Wild Draw Four can be played only when you hold no card of the active color.
  Strict UNO (the default) gives you a few seconds to call UNO when you have one
  card left. Missing it draws two cards. Casual mode calls UNO for you.
  Classic scoring races to 100, 250, or 500. Number cards score their face value,
  action cards 20, and wild cards 50.
  If the draw pile empties, the discard is shuffled without the active card.
  If a Draw Two or Wild Draw Four is the last card, the next player still draws
  before the round is scored.

Keys
  Left and Right select a card, Enter plays it, D draws, K keeps a draw,
  U calls UNO, 1–4 choose a wild color, Esc opens the leave prompt.
